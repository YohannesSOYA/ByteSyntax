## 📋 1. Presentation Outline (CodeFusion 2026 Edition)

### **Introduction & Theme**
- **Theme:** Daily-Life (College Parcel Collection).
- **The Vision:** "Modernizing the campus experience for students and staff through digital tracking."

### **The Problem (Why we built this):**
- **Manual Logging:** Paper notebooks are slow and lose data.
- **Verification Issues:** Hard to prove exactly who collected a parcel without a digital audit trail.
- **Wasted Time:** Staff spend hours searching for boxes; students wait in long lines.

### **The Solution (Objectives):**
- **Admin Hub:** Securely register and manage parcel records.
- **Student Tracker:** 3-factor lookup (Name + Phone + Last 4 Tracking Digits).
- **Real-Time Status:** Instantly see if an item is "Arrived" vs "Collected".

### **Technical Logic (The IPO Model):**
- **Input:** Student details, tracking numbers, and login credentials.
- **Process:** Secure authentication, record registration, and status updates.
- **Output:** Successful registration alerts, "Item Found" notifications, and status reports.

### **🚀 EXCEEDING EXPECTATIONS: Going Beyond the Proposal**
*In our proposal, we listed these as limitations, but for our prototype, we actually built them!*
1. **Email Notifications:** We added a secure email-based password recovery system for admins.
2. **Scanner Integration:** We implemented a QR scanner for contactless, error-free collection.
